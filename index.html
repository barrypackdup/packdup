About

Why PackdUp exists

PackdUp is the home of backpacker travel — built on real firsthand experience across 37 countries, 6 continents and over 300 days on the road.

Most travel content online is scraped, recycled and written by people who've never been. PackdUp is different. Every Verified destination is somewhere I've actually been — slept in the hostels, eaten the food, made the mistakes so you don't have to.

National Geographic meets your mate who's actually done the trip.

The AI tools aren't generic. They're built on a backbone of nearly 175,000 words of firsthand travel writing — real prices, real hostel names, real honest verdicts. When PackdUp tells you a place is worth it, it's because I've been there. When it tells you to skip something, same.
